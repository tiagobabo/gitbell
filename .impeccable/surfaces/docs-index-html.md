## Scope

The GitBell product page, `docs/index.html`, served by GitHub Pages. Mode: Persuade. Visitors are engineers on busy teams arriving from GitHub, a colleague's link or a post; the action is downloading the DMG. They should leave believing GitBell tells them what to do next. Proof is the real app (demo-data screenshots) and the real mechanism shown in the page itself; no invented testimonials, numbers or claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A sign tells you where to go next; GitBell tells you what to do next. The page is a station's signage system, not a dev-tool landing page: it refuses the dark glow hero over a screenshot with a bento grid of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: White tile-wall ground; black enamel sign bands run edge to edge at full strength; white Aileron (self-hosted neo-grotesque) set tight and bold; colour lives only in round line bullets, one per action (Review R orange, Re-review P yellow, Fix F red, Failed after merge X brown, On hold H purple, Reply C blue, Merge M green, Mergeable G lime) and in a yellow platform-edge strip. Text fields stay black and white. Waiting time sits in a fixed tabular field on every item.

STORY: The visitor sees their own morning on a sign: what's waiting, why, and for how long. They learn every action has a line, their pull requests run along a line map to production, everything has a key, and setup is three honest steps. They download.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A thin black station band nav (icon and GitBell left, Features, Install, Source right). Below, a full-bleed black sign: "Your turn" at display scale, a row of line bullets with counts, then a departure list of four demo pull requests (bullet, title, repo#, reason, waiting time right-aligned). The Download for Mac exit sign sits in the sign's top right at sign-plate scale, with version and macOS 14 beneath. The yellow platform edge closes the viewport with the app screenshot rising behind it.

FORM: Transit wayfinding, the 1970 subway signage manual's grammar; candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:7cd8f2afbfb105e9. Signature interaction: the hero's line bullets are buttons that filter the departure list to that line. Motion grammar: rows change like a sign updating, a short vertical flip with exponential ease-out, once per change; nothing else animates on scroll. Raises: draft to deployed is one line map with stations in fixed order (darkroom); colour only in bullets and strip edges (cloud edge); one instruction per sign (linocut); waiting time on every item (brine); black band owned edge to edge (single hue).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
